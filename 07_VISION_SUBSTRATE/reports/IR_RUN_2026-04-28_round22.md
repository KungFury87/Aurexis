## First run: Phone photos folder (13 images)

Ran the audit on Vincent's phone photos library. ~18 seconds total
(1.4 s per image x 13). Output: PHOXELIS_AUDIT_phone_photos.md +
.csv at workspace root.

### Headline findings (no human input - language detected on its own)

  100% indoor_scene_signature           - all 13 are indoor shots
  100% specular_highlights               - eyes/glints/screens/glass
  100% significant_orange_hue            - warm indoor lighting
  92% significant_blue_hue                - cool light leak (window, screens)
  85% center_weighted_lighting           - subject centred portrait convention
  77% genuine_text_not_screen            - many shots are printed-text content
  38% human_subject_signature             - 5 photos with face-like signal
  31% face_like_signature                 - 4 photos
  23% screen_displaying_text              - 3 photos OF a screen
  15% face_is_dominant_concept            - 2 photos with face dominating

### Time-of-day clustering the language surfaced

  Morning (07:19-07:40):  face_like + screen_displaying_face + blue_dominant
  Afternoon (13:46-14:39): text_like + perspective_convergence + lower-left subject
  Evening (19:53):        screen_dominant + screen_displaying_text

The clustering matches what humans would describe about the photos.
